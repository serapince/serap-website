    About me

    I'm Serap. I grew up wanting to see what was past the next hill, and I still do. I travel slowly, with a notebook and a camera, and I come home with more questions than souvenirs. Between trips I dive, sail, take photos and bake. This site is where all of it lives.

    Outside of work you'll find me on long walks with no destination, cooking for too many people, or arguing about the best window seat on a night train.

    I'm happiest somewhere I can't read the street signs yet.
